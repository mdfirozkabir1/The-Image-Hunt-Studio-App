About The Image Hunt Studio

Hunt for Perfection.

Hunt for Perfection.

Careful edits, delivered on time.

The Image Hunt Studio is an experienced photo editing team that works with online stores, photographers and agencies. We cut, clean, retouch and prepare images so they are ready to publish.

We started with one simple idea: product photos should sell the product, not distract from it. Years of hands-on work with clipping paths, masking, ghost mannequin and retouching have taught us what a clean, marketplace-ready image really looks like.

Every path is drawn by hand with the pen tool. Every file is checked before it leaves us. That is why brands keep sending us their next batch.
